Universal Orlando Resort FAQs
Is Universal Epic Universe open?
Five immersive worlds. One amazing theme park. Step through extraordinary portals transporting you to the excitement of Universal Epic Universe theme park, now open at Universal Orlando Resort. Select multi-day tickets are on sale now. Plus, bookings are also available for Universal&rsquo;s newest hotel, the spectacular Universal Helios Grand Hotel, a Loews Hotel.
Are reservations required to visit Universal Orlando Resort?
Reservations are not required to attend Universal Orlando Resort&rsquo;s theme parks or Universal CityWalk. However, reservations are strongly recommended for hotel stays and select dining experiences.
How does Universal Express Pass work?
Universal Express Pass access is available as an optional add-on that empowers you to skip the regular lines at select rides and attractions at Universal Orlando Resort theme parks. Participating attractions and rides are subject to change without notice.
How far is Universal Orlando Resort from International Drive and downtown Orlando?
Universal Orlando Resort is on the north end of Orlando&rsquo;s International Drive Entertainment District. It is about 15 minutes south of downtown Orlando.
