Orlando Theme Park FAQs
How many theme parks are there in Orlando?
No destination on Earth has more &mdash; or more iconic &mdash; theme parks than Orlando. We have 10 theme parks, a water theme park and multiple water parks.
How many theme parks are at WALT DISNEY WORLD&reg; Resort?
WALT DISNEY WORLD has four theme parks, including MAGIC KINGDOM® Park , EPCOT ®, DISNEY’S HOLLYWOOD STUDIOS ® and DISNEY’S ANIMAL KINGDOM® Theme Park . They also have two water parks: DISNEY’S BLIZZARD BEACH Water Park  and DISNEY’S TYPHOON LAGOON Water Park .  With so many imaginative options to choose from, as well as dining, shopping and fun at DISNEY SPRINGS ®, and more than two dozen official hotel properties and other accommodations , you will never run out of unforgettable, memory-making adventures at WALT DISNEY WORLD. ( Reservations may be required at the time of your visit .)
How many theme parks are at Universal Orlando Resort?
Universal Orlando has four parks, including a water theme park. They include Universal Studios Florida , Universal Islands of Adventure , Universal Volcano Bay and the all-new  Universal Epic Universe . You can also opt for dinner, entertainment, shopping and more at Universal CityWalk , located at the nexus of Universal Orlando, or an amazing stay at one of Universal Orlando’s official hotel properties and other nearby accommodations .
How many theme parks are at SeaWorld&reg; Orlando?
For up-close animal encounters and pulse-pounding rides, SeaWorld’s main Orlando theme park has you covered. You can also take advantage of sandy beaches and thrilling rides at Aquatica® Orlando , or enjoy serene relaxation, awe-inspiring animal encounters and perfect pampering at Discovery Cove ® ( reservations required ). SeaWorld is also an official partner of several nearby hotels , which provide guests with special park benefits.
How many theme parks are at LEGOLAND&reg; Florida Resort?
Children and their families can have a blast with kids-focused rides and plenty of building opportunities at LEGOLAND Florida Resort. In addition to LEGOLAND® Florida Theme Park and its onsite LEGOLAND® Florida Water Park , LEGOLAND Florida operates Peppa Pig Theme Park Florida and SEA LIFE Florida Aquarium.
