Things to Do in Orlando FAQs
Is there anything fun to do in Orlando?
Yes, starting with our world-famous theme parks and continuing through all the other things there are to do here. In fact, it would take more than 121 days to fully enjoy all of Orlando&rsquo;s unforgettable experiences.
Explore
Is there anything to do in Orlando besides theme parks?
Yes. In addition to standalone attractions, you can enjoy award-winning dining, world-class arts, authentic neighborhoods, outdoor adventures for all ages and interests, live sports, concerts, nightlife, and much more in Orlando.
Explore
What to do in Orlando for two days?
Two days in Orlando is plenty of time to enjoy a theme park or two. Fill out your itinerary with acclaimed dining, live music and performing arts, professional sports, neighborhood exploration and more.
How to spend three hours in Orlando?
Orlando&rsquo;s standalone attractions, including our acclaimed museums and galleries, are perfect for filling a few hours. Most are in our International Drive Entertainment District (I-Drive), but you will find more throughout the destination.
Explore
What to do in Orlando on a budget?
Orlando has things to do for any budget, including free things to do and 30+ things to do for $30  or less. You can save even more with special offers and authorized discount theme park and attraction tickets from Visit Orlando powered by BestofOrlando.com.
What is Orlando best known for?
As the Theme Park Capital of the World, Orlando is best known for its theme parks. However, it is also a go-to destination for culinary tourism, outdoor adventures, concerts, live sports, vibrant arts and much more &mdash; not to mention year-round sunny skies.
