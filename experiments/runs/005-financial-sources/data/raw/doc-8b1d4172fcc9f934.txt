Corporate Profile
EHang is a pilotless aerial vehicle technology platform company. Our mission is to make safe, pilotless and eco-friendly air mobility accessible to everyone.
In today’s increasingly populated and interconnected world, traditional modes of urban transportation continue to contribute to congestion and pollution, and they are largely confined to land-based infrastructure. We are pioneering the future of transportation through our proprietarily developed pilotless aerial vehicles, or pilotless eVTOL aircraft, and related commercial solutions for urban air mobility including passenger transportation and logistics, smart city management and aerial media solutions.
